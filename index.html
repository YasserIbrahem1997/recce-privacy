Recce is a production marketplace operated by Code Solution (“we”, “us”, or “our”). It connects clients who hire creative crews with providers (talent, studios, and gear). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account data: mobile phone number (for OTP sign-in), and profile details you provide (name, role, bio, portfolio links, skills).
  • Production data: briefs, titles, quantities, dates, city/country, notes, bids, bookings, and messages related to a job.
  • Files: photos and other deliverables you upload (stored securely, for example via Firebase Storage).
  • Payments & escrow: transaction and funding information needed to post jobs, hold funds, release payouts, and manage bid credits or fees.
  • Device & technical data: device type, OS, app version, IP address, crash/diagnostic logs, and push notification tokens.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, we send updates about bids, bookings, deliveries, and payments.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you and operate your account
  • Match clients with providers and run productions end-to-end
  • Process payments, escrow, fees, and credits
  • Send transactional notifications
  • Prevent fraud, improve reliability, and comply with law

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Sharing

We share data only as needed to run the App:

  • With the other party in a production (for example brief details, city, deliverables, reviews)
  • With service providers such as Firebase (Auth, Messaging, Storage), hosting/API providers, and payment processors
  • When required by law or to protect users and the platform

Retention & security

We keep account and transaction data while your account is active and as required for legal, accounting, and dispute purposes. We use HTTPS and industry-standard safeguards; no system is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Update profile information in the App
  • Disable notifications in device settings
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing yasserelabasy1997@gmail.com

Children

Recce is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
